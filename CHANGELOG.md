## [v0.1.0] - 2026-05-31 ([`a11b3b8`](https://github.com/plexusone/omni-newrelic/commit/a11b3b8))

### Highlights

- New Relic integrations for PlexusOne omnisignal and omniobserve abstraction layers
- Signal provider for alert and incident ingestion via omnisignal
- Observability provider with telemetry export and data query capabilities
